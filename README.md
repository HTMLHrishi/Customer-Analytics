# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer purchasing patterns, product preferences, spending behavior, and subscription trends using Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **3,900 customer transactions** across different product categories to uncover meaningful business insights. The objective is to understand how customers shop, identify purchasing trends, evaluate subscription behavior, and support data-driven business decisions.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, starting with data cleaning and feature engineering in Python, followed by database integration, SQL-based business analysis, and interactive dashboard development in Power BI.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **SQL:** Data analysis, aggregations, joins, window functions, and customer segmentation
- **Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI
- **Libraries:** SQLAlchemy, Psycopg2

## 📂 Dataset Overview

- **Total Records:** 3,900
- **Total Columns:** 18
- **Missing Values:** 37 in the Review Rating column

Key information includes:
- Customer demographics: Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status
- Purchase details: Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color
- Shopping behavior: Discount Applied, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Shipping Type

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation (Python)

- Loaded and explored the dataset using Pandas.
- Examined data types, descriptive statistics, and missing values.
- Imputed missing review ratings using the median rating of each product category.
- Standardized column names using lowercase and snake_case formatting.
- Created age groups using `pd.qcut()`.
- Converted purchase frequency into equivalent days using dictionary mapping.
- Checked for redundancy between discount and promo code fields and removed the redundant column.

### 2. Database Integration (PostgreSQL)

- Connected Python to PostgreSQL using SQLAlchemy and Psycopg2.
- Loaded the cleaned DataFrame into a PostgreSQL database.
- Created the customer table for further SQL-based analysis.

### 3. SQL Business Analysis

Performed SQL queries to answer important business questions:

1. Revenue by Gender – Compared revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. High-Spending Discount Users – Identified customers who used discounts but spent above the average purchase amount.
3. Top 5 Products by Rating – Identified products with the highest average review ratings.
4. Shipping Type Comparison – Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.
5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers – Analyzed average spending and total revenue across subscription groups.
6. Discount-Dependent Products – Identified products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.
7. Customer Segmentation – Classified customers into New, Returning, and Loyal segments based on purchase history.
8. Top 3 Products per Category – Identified the most purchased products within each category.
9. Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions – Examined the relationship between repeat purchases and subscription status.
10. Revenue by Age Group – Analyzed revenue contribution across customer age groups.

### 4. Power BI Dashboard

Developed an interactive dashboard to visualize key business metrics and customer shopping patterns.

**Dashboard Highlights:**
- Total Customers
- Average Review Rating
- Average Purchase Amount
- Customer Subscription Distribution
- Revenue by Product Category
- Sales by Product Category
- Revenue by Age Group
- Sales by Age Group

Interactive filters include subscription status, gender, category, and shipping type.

## 📊 Key Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits to encourage more customers to subscribe.
- **Customer Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat buyers and strengthen customer retention.
- **Review Discount Policies:** Balance discount-driven sales with profitability.
- **Product Positioning:** Highlight top-rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns.
- **Targeted Marketing:** Focus on high-revenue customer segments and relevant shipping preferences.
